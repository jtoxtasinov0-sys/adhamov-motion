# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS in one page (no build step), so a non-technical owner can open, edit text and host it anywhere.

## Users

Uzbek-speaking people who want to earn with motion design. Two groups: complete beginners who have never opened After Effects, and people who already know After Effects basics and want to make SaaS / product animations for clients.

## Product Purpose

A landing page that sells Asilbek Adhamov's two online motion design courses and sends buyers to Telegram to enrol. Success = visitor writes to @adhamov_motion.

## Positioning

Taught by a working motion editor whose own client-grade animations are shown on the page — the teacher's reel is the proof.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Course 1: Motion design from zero — $100 (for beginners).
- Course 2: SaaS animation — $200 (for those who already know After Effects).
- Enrolment: Telegram @adhamov_motion (https://t.me/adhamov_motion). Channel: https://t.me/adhamovmotion.
- Module lists, durations and lesson counts are not confirmed by the owner (inferred: drafted by Claude, owner will edit).
- Page language: Uzbek (Latin).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Asilbek Adhamov.
- User-pinned references: orange + black "creative director" portfolio look; portrait on saturated orange background. The page must feel professional with smooth, "wow" animation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portrait: assets/asilbek.webp
- Work samples from Telegram posts adhamovmotion/389, /388, /374 (short videos, 12–17s): assets/work/
- Testimonials: none real yet. Placeholder reviews are drafted by Claude and must be replaced by the owner before launch.

## Product Principles

1. Show the motion, don't describe it — the page itself must move like his work.
2. Two clear doors: beginner or After Effects user; never confuse the two.
3. One action: write on Telegram.
